# Privacy Policy for Finance Tracker

**Last Updated: August 18, 2025**

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the Finance Tracker mobile application ("App").

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** When you sign up, we collect your email address and password to create and secure your account.
- **Financial Data:** We collect the financial information you provide, such as transactions, budgets, and categories. This data is essential for the App's core functionality.
- **Device Information:** We may collect information about the device you use to access the App, including the hardware model, operating system and version, and unique device identifiers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the App.
- Personalize your experience.
- Enable features like cloud backup and data synchronization.
- Communicate with you, including sending you service-related announcements.
- Secure your account and data.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using third-party cloud services (e.g., Firebase). We take reasonable measures to help protect your information from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

## 4. Cloud Backup

If you choose to use the cloud backup feature, your financial data will be stored on third-party servers. This allows you to restore your data if you change devices. By using this feature, you agree to the privacy policies of the third-party service provider.

## 5. Biometric Authentication

The App offers the option to use your device's biometric authentication (e.g., fingerprint, Face ID) for logging in. We do not collect, store, or have access to your biometric data. This information is managed by your device's operating system.

## 6. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy, such as with our third-party service providers who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting).

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through the App's settings. If you delete your account, we will also delete your personal information.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
